How It Works

From your daily habits to your predicted GWA. Explore what the estimator measures and how it produces your result.

What goes into the prediction?

The estimator uses five values from your daily routine:

  • Average daily screen time
  • Academic phone use during study time
  • Social media or entertainment phone use during study time
  • Average study hours per day
  • Study frequency per week

How is my GWA estimated?

Your inputs feed a Linear Regression model that estimates your General Weighted Average (GWA) on the 1.00–5.00 scale. A lower GWA indicates better academic performance.

What does my result mean?

Your predicted GWA maps to one of four performance bands:

Excellent
1.00–1.75
Good
1.76–2.50
Satisfactory
2.51–3.00
Failing
3.01–5.00

What should I know about the model?

This prototype uses a Linear Regression model trained on synthetic data. It can be retrained when real survey data becomes available.

Use the result to reflect on your habits. It is an estimate, not an official grade.
