How It Works

From your daily habits to your predicted GWA. Explore what the estimator measures and how it produces your result.

What goes into the prediction?

The estimator uses:

  • Average daily screen time
  • Program and year level
  • The additional study and phone-use answers do not affect the current estimate.

How is my GWA estimated?

Your inputs feed a ridge regression model that estimates your General Weighted Average (GWA) on the 1.00–5.00 scale. A lower GWA indicates better academic performance.

What does my result mean?

Your predicted GWA maps to one of four performance bands:

Excellent
1.00–1.75
Good
1.76–2.50
Satisfactory
2.51–3.00
Failing
3.01–5.00

What should I know about the model?

This ridge regression model was trained on CISTM survey responses to estimate a completed semester GWA. It has not been validated to predict future grades.

Screen time and study habits were recorded after the completed semester used as the target.

Use the result to reflect on your habits. It is an estimate, not an official grade.
