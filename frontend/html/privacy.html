2. Information processed

  • Assessment inputs: your screen time, academic and social phone use, study hours and days, year level, program, and academic term.
  • Assessment results: predicted GWA, performance category, and suggestions. Saved history also includes the assessment date and record identifiers.
  • Sign-in information: Microsoft account identifiers, display name, email when available, and account creation and last sign-in times. Your Microsoft password is entered with Microsoft; this app does not ask for or store it.
  • Technical information: session cookies and server-side session records, your theme preference and unfinished assessment drafts in browser storage, and cross-tab account notifications. Drafts contain your entered answers and assessment step; they are kept separately for guests and each signed-in account on this browser. The web server or hosting provider may also process request metadata such as IP address, browser information, and request time.

3. Why and how information is used

Your submitted habits are sent to the server to calculate a GWA estimate using a regression model. The estimate determines a performance category and suggestions. See How It Works for the inputs and performance bands.

Sign-in information links saved assessments to your account and controls access to your history. Technical information supports sessions, security, error handling, and your display preference.

4. Guest use and saved history

You can request an estimate without signing in. Guest submissions are processed to return a result but are not added to account history. When you are signed in, successful assessments are saved to your account automatically.

Unfinished answers are automatically saved and restored on this browser. Guest drafts remain separate when you sign in and are not transferred to your account. Reset or Start again discards the current draft. Signing out deletes that account's draft on this browser and clears its answers in other open tabs. Drafts are not synchronized across devices.

You can review your saved assessments in History, delete an individual assessment, or clear all assessment history. Clearing history does not delete your account profile or your Microsoft account. To correct an assessment, delete the incorrect record and submit a new one.

Microsoft processes sign-in requests under its own privacy terms. Some app pages load fonts from Google Fonts and styling software from the Tailwind CDN; those providers receive the network requests needed to deliver those resources. Opening a configured social link takes you to a separate service.

Authorized operators and service providers may need access to stored information to maintain the application, database, and backups. 6. Retention and security

Sign-in sessions expire eight hours after sign-in. Visits do not extend this period. Signing out revokes this browser's session, and clearing its cookies ends the session. Expired server-side session records are removed by cleanup tasks.

Unfinished drafts expire 24 hours after the last answer change. Expired drafts are removed when the application next checks browser storage; they are not deleted in the background while the browser is closed. Completed guest assessments and assessments successfully saved to history clear their matching drafts. A signed-in draft is retained if saving fails so you can retry. Clearing site data removes browser drafts and preferences. A saved theme preference otherwise remains until you change it.

Saved assessments remain available until deleted; the current app does not apply an automatic age-based assessment or account deletion schedule. Deleted assessments may remain in backups. The deployment plan specifies a backup retention period of up to 30 days, which the operator must confirm is implemented.
