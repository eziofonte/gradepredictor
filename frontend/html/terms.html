2. Educational purpose

The app estimates a student's General Weighted Average (GWA) from self-reported phone usage and study habits. It is intended to support reflection on study routines.

Results are prototype estimates, not official grades, academic records, or guarantees of future performance. Use official university records and qualified academic guidance for decisions about your education. An estimate must not be used as the sole basis for admission, grading, scholarships, disciplinary action, or other decisions about a student's opportunities.

3. Responsible use

  • Use your own information, or information you are authorized to provide, and enter values as accurately as you can.
  • Use only an account you are authorized to access. Keep your sign-in credentials private and sign out on shared devices.
  • Do not attempt to access another person's history, bypass security controls, overload the service, or disrupt its operation.
  • Do not present a prediction as an official university result or misrepresent the app's approval or affiliation.

4. Accounts and history

Guest estimates are available without saved account history. Microsoft sign-in enables history, and successful assessments submitted while signed in are saved automatically. You can review or delete assessments from History.

Keep your own copy of any result you need. The prototype is not an official academic archive. Account deletion and other privacy requests should be directed to the contact listed in the Privacy notice.

5. Accuracy and availability

Estimates depend on the submitted inputs and the model's limitations. They may be inaccurate and cannot capture every factor affecting academic performance. A relationship between phone use, study habits, and an estimate does not establish that one factor causes a particular grade.

Features and availability may change, and interruptions or errors may occur. The prototype does not promise uninterrupted access, a particular result, or an improvement in grades. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.
