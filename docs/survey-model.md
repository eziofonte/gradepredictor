# Survey cleaning and exploratory GWA model

Run `venv\Scripts\python.exe survey_training.py "PATH_TO_WORKBOOK.xlsx"` after installing `requirements.txt`. Results go to `output/survey_model/`. The workbook is read only. Keep the output private: removing names and emails does not make grades and survey responses anonymous, and source row numbers can link records back to the original file. Do not commit or share the data or model without the research team's approval.

The script reads all three sheets, includes only responses explicitly indicating consent and CISTM enrollment, and drops names, email, timestamps, evidence links, course-level ratings and other unused fields from its output. It does not verify uploaded evidence or determine whether two records belong to the same person. The fourth-year sheets lack a common respondent identifier, so cross-sheet duplicates cannot be reliably resolved. Confirm provenance and duplicate handling with the research team.

The target is the most recent valid **completed regular-semester** official GWA (numeric 1–5); midyear is omitted because it may represent a different course load. A reported value is accepted only when its semester is marked completed. A later semester with an invalid or missing value is skipped; a previous valid completed semester may be selected. Inspect `target_term` when reviewing this decision. Respondents without a valid target, recognized program or year are excluded. Other survey fields are not used to reconstruct a GWA.

Screen time uses the survey's separate numeric average-daily-hours question, constrained to 0–24. Each of the four study-habit features is the mean of five 1–5 Likert items, requiring at least four valid responses. Missing feature values are imputed using the training split only, with missingness indicators. Program and year are one-hot encoded; the regression model is ridge regression. All preprocessing is in the saved scikit-learn pipeline. A fixed, program-and-year-stratified 80/20 split is used; the saved model is trained on the 80% training portion, and the test portion was held out for the metrics. Compare against the training-set mean baseline. `metrics.json` includes sample counts, missingness, MAE, RMSE, R² and small-group MAE. Results from a single split are uncertain and not external validation. Exact matching of selected variables is only a duplicate signal, not a person-level duplicate check.

The observed screen-time week is later than the completed semester being estimated, and study habits are self-reported. This is a retrospective association exercise, not a prospective prediction, causal finding, or a basis for decisions about individual students. Verify dates, response validity, grades, semester alignment and representativeness with the researchers; gather habits and screen time before a future semester's grade to evaluate true forecasting. No causal importance is implied by the model.

The web application's existing fields are different from these survey measures. This pipeline is deliberately not connected to it; update its questionnaire and validate the resulting workflow before deploying predictions. The existing synthetic-data model remains separate.
